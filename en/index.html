¡appa! GIFT

¡appa! GIFT · multi-brand digital gift card

¡appa! GIFT is a digital gift card stored in the app. Users get it as a reward, through in-app loyalty programs, repeat-purchase campaigns or corporate (B2B) purchases, and redeem it at participating stores nationwide.

GIFTMulti-brand digital gift card
  • New customers¡appa! users who can choose your brand to spend their GIFT.
  • Store visitsThe GIFT is redeemed in person at participating stores.
  • In-app balanceUsers carry the balance on their phone and spend it whenever they want.
  • Payment methodIt can cover part of a purchase at your brand.

Repeat purchase at your own brand

A mechanic that encourages people who already bought from your brand to come back and buy again, using an ¡appa! GIFT.

  1. 1PurchaseThe user buys something at your store.
  2. 2Gets a GIFTThe store hands them a self-claim QR code: once scanned, the ¡appa! GIFT is loaded into their app.
  3. 3New purchaseThey can use the GIFT on their next purchase at your brand.
